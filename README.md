## 🎮 About the Game

Screw Drivers is a vehicle-building racing simulation developed by Creactstudios and published by Headup. It launched in Early Access on June 20, 2024, and received its full release on August 20, 2026. Players construct cars, trucks, tanks, planes, and other machines from building blocks, engines, axles, gears, suspensions, wheels, and propellers, then race them in physics-driven challenges.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Screw Drivers**. This trainer enhances vehicle construction, progression, and racing in Screw Drivers. It provides optional controls for unlocking mechanical parts, adjusting vehicle physics, managing race conditions, and recovering from difficult crashes while preserving the game's core building and driving systems.

**Target Audience:** Players who enjoy experimenting with complex drivetrains, testing unconventional vehicles, unlocking construction parts quickly, or reducing the time required to replay difficult races.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Movement is vehicle-based rather than character-based. Build a drivetrain with engines, axles, gears, suspensions, and wheels, then use the driving controls to steer, accelerate, brake, and test the vehicle's handling. Trainer physics and recovery options are most useful while evaluating unstable designs or vehicles with unusual wheel layouts.

### Tools & Inventory

Screw Drivers uses a construction toolkit and parts collection instead of a traditional character inventory. Vehicle components are selected in the build editor and assembled into the chassis, drivetrain, suspension, and propulsion system. The unlock option makes available parts accessible without completing the related progression challenges first.

### World & Environment

The game features an island environment with tracks, races, challenges, and multiplayer areas. Completing challenges and defeating royal opponents normally unlocks additional engines, wheels, gears, and construction pieces. Use the trainer in single-player activities when exploring tracks or testing vehicles outside normal progression.

### Combat & Weapons

Screw Drivers does not center on conventional character combat. Its primary activities are vehicle construction, physics experimentation, racing, crashes, and challenge completion. Although the editor supports imaginative builds such as tanks and other machines, the trainer is intended for racing, vehicle testing, and progression rather than combat encounters.
